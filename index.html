Process

Four steps. No vaporware.

Scroll-build preview — a stand-in for a Google Flow scroll animation. The real version needs actual frames of a site being built.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    A 30-minute conversation. We audit what you have, learn what you actually need, confirm access requirements, and follow up with a written scope, flat price, and realistic schedule.

  2. 02

    Design in the open

    You see the site take shape in a live preview link — not a static mockup. Feedback in plain English, no design jargon.

  3. 03

    Ship & measure

    After your approval, we launch the agreed changes on your domain and provide a short handoff walkthrough. Analytics are added when included in your scope.

  4. 04

    Scoped aftercare

    Your proposal defines a limited correction window for the agreed work. New requests are quoted separately, with an optional care plan for ongoing support.
